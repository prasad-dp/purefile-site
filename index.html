Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about PureFile's technology and privacy.

Are my files ever uploaded to any server or cloud?
No. Absolutely never. PureFile does not maintain or operate any file conversion servers. All PDF rendering, merging, splitting, image conversions, and encryption execute 100% locally on your phone’s processor.
Does PureFile work without an internet connection?
Yes. PureFile is engineered to work seamlessly in Airplane Mode. You can convert images, merge multi-gigabyte PDFs, sign contracts, and access your encrypted vault with zero Wi-Fi or cellular connection.
What permissions does PureFile require?
Zero dangerous permissions. PureFile does not request camera, contacts, microphone, or location access. It uses Android's modern Scoped Storage Document Picker, meaning it only accesses the specific files you tap to open.
How does the Private Vault protect my files?
Vault files are encrypted using military-grade AES-256 GCM. The encryption keys are anchored directly into your phone’s Android StrongBox/Keystore hardware module, and are only accessible following a valid fingerprint or Face ID match.
Is PureFile completely free?
Yes! PureFile offers generous free limits for all offline file tools. An optional PureFile Pro upgrade is available via Google Play for users handling massive file batches or wanting a 100% ad-free experience.
